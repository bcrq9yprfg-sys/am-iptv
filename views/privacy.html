Privacy Policy

AM IPTV — website and Samsung TV app · Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

AM IPTV is operated by AM Consul, Den Haag, the Netherlands. Contact: support@amiptv.tv.

2. What AM IPTV is

AM IPTV is a media player. It does not provide, sell or host any TV channels, films or series. You add your own playlist (M3U link or Xtream login) from a provider you choose.

3. Data we process

  • Account (website): name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Subscription: chosen plan, license code, start and end date, and the Stripe subscription ID.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never receive or store your card details. Stripe may collect your billing address.
  • TV app license check: the app sends your license code to our server to check that it is valid.
  • Free trial: to give each TV one 3-day free trial, the app sends a device identifier (Samsung DUID, or a random ID created by the app) to our server. We store it with the trial start date.
  • Adding a playlist with your phone (QR code): the playlist link or Xtream login you enter on your phone is stored on our server only until your TV picks it up, and is then deleted. Unused entries are deleted within about an hour.
  • Cookies (website): a login cookie and a language cookie. No advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Data that stays on your TV

Your playlist link, Xtream login, favourites, watch history and subtitle settings are stored on your TV. They are not sent to us, except briefly when you use the QR code (see above). The app connects directly to the playlist provider you entered. If online subtitles are enabled, the title and language of what you watch are sent to OpenSubtitles.com to find subtitles.

5. Why we use your data

Only to provide your account, process payments, issue and verify your license, and give support. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligations such as tax records (art. 6(1)(c)). We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

6. Service providers

  • Stripe — payments
  • Railway — server hosting (may be located outside the EU; transfers are covered by standard contractual clauses)

7. How long we keep data

Account data: until you delete your account. Device identifiers for the free trial: as long as the service exists, to prevent repeated trials. Payment and invoice records: 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to processing, by emailing support@amiptv.tv. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

9. Children

AM IPTV is not intended for children under 16.

Privacybeleid

Nederlandse samenvatting

AM IPTV (AM Consul, Den Haag) is een mediaspeler en levert zelf geen zenders, films of series. Wij verwerken alleen je naam, e-mailadres, wachtwoord (versleuteld opgeslagen), abonnement en licentiecode. Betalingen lopen via Stripe; wij zien geen kaartgegevens. De TV-app stuurt je licentiecode naar ons ter controle, en een toestel-ID zodat elke TV één keer 3 dagen gratis kan proberen. Je playlist, inloggegevens, favorieten en kijkgeschiedenis blijven op je TV; alleen als je de QR-code gebruikt, staat je playlist kort op onze server tot je TV hem heeft opgehaald, daarna wordt hij gewist. We verkopen niets door en gebruiken geen advertentie- of trackingcookies. Factuurgegevens bewaren we 7 jaar (fiscale plicht). Inzage, correctie of verwijdering: support@amiptv.tv. Klachten kun je indienen bij de Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
